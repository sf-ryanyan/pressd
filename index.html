Applying and removing

Four steps · About ten minutes
01

Size

Lay the set beside your hands and hold each tip against the nail before you commit. The tip should sit just inside the side walls without pressing into the skin. If you are between sizes, take the smaller one — a tip that overhangs will catch and lift within a day.

02

Prepare

Push the cuticle back with the wooden stick, buff the surface until the shine is gone, then wipe each nail with an alcohol pad and let it dry for thirty seconds. Skip this and you will get three days instead of ten — oil on the nail plate is the single biggest cause of early lifting.

03

Apply

Choose your adhesive: tabs for three to five days and easy removal, or a thin line of gel adhesive for up to fourteen days. Press from the cuticle outward at a slight angle to push the air out, then hold firm for thirty seconds. Avoid water for two hours.

04

Remove

Soak in warm soapy water for ten minutes, then work a cuticle stick under the edge and lift slowly from the side, not the tip. Peel the adhesive residue off the back of the tip and the set is ready to wear again — most sets last three to four wears.

Never pull a set off dry. Press-on nails adhere to the top layer of the natural nail, and forcing one off takes that layer with it. If a nail resists after soaking, soak it again — it will always release eventually. Discontinue use if redness, swelling or irritation occurs, and keep adhesive away from children and pets.
